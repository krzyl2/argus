<objective>
Half A of Phase 9 (mechanical, config-validation + static-copy layer). Lower the group member-count
floor from 3 to 2 at the three documented config-validation enforcement points, switch the guided
chooser's "together" recommendation from ecod to copod, and rewrite all 5 DetectorCatalog BestFor
copy entries.

Purpose: Unblock 2-member groups at the save/load boundary (GRP-10, GRP-12) — without this, a
2-member group can never be saved or loaded regardless of how well the detector side is built — and
correct the empirically-wrong guided default and misleading catalog copy (ALGO-05, ALGO-06).
Output: Floor-of-2 validation (both modes), copod guided default, rewritten BestFor copy, updated
tests.
</objective>
