<objective>
Half B (C# orchestrator wiring). Make the five count-blind, Mode-string-only branches in the .NET
orchestrator count-aware / response-shape-aware, so a 2-member peer_divergence group actually gets
fitted, scored, published, and retracted correctly as a single group-level relationship check.

Purpose: Without these fixes, a correctly-configured 2-member peer_divergence group silently
misbehaves — never fitted (Pitfall 5), skipped every stale tick (Pitfall 1), publishes nothing
(Pitfall 2), or creates two meaningless per-member entities (Pitfalls 3-4). Completes GRP-11's
end-to-end delivery and GRP-12's runtime-floor consistency.
Output: Count-aware BatchSchedulerWorker (3 edits), a shared `UsesPerMemberEntities` helper on
DiscoveryPublisher reused by MqttPublisherWorker, and updated/new C# tests. No new symbols leak
pairwise-delta math into C#; every fix replaces a Mode-string check with a count/shape check.
</objective>

<assumptions>
- **[ASSUMED, low-risk] 2-member peer_divergence publishes ONE group-level MQTT/HA entity pair**
  (binary_sensor + sensor, memberId=null) — RESEARCH.md Assumption A2 / Open Design Question #5.
  This matches the `group_verdict` response shape from Plan 09-02 and the fact that a pairwise delta
  cannot attribute to either member. Operator-revisable and isolated to the single
  `UsesPerMemberEntities(group)` helper — flip its `>= 3` predicate (and 09-02's response shape) to
  switch to per-member entities later.
- **[ASSUMED, low-risk] Config-validation floor is 2 for both modes** (Assumption A1, implemented in
  Plan 09-01). This plan's BuildGroupMatrix change (gate the drop-stale-then-require-floor policy on
  `group.Members.Count >= 3`) is the runtime counterpart: 2-member peer groups use the
  skip-whole-group-on-staleness policy, which is the only correct policy when a pairwise delta needs
  both members present.
</assumptions>
