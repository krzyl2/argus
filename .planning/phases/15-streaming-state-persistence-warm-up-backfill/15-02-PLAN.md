<objective>
Make the detector the single source of truth for warm-up and get per-entity `hst` params onto the wire.
Adds three `Verdict` fields plus a `Point.params` map, forwards those params through
`servicer.ScoreStream`, and converts `EntityRuntimeState` from a self-incrementing counter into a
verdict-driven projection.

Purpose: closes defects D2 (`EntityRuntimeState._readingCount` is a second independent counter, reset on
every `RunAsync`) and D3 (`score_one` called without params, so a configured `window: 50` never reaches
HST) from the ROADMAP defect table.

Output: a wire contract carrying warm-up truth, and an orchestrator that reads it instead of counting.
</objective>
