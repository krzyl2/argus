Rebuild the detector editor: replace the `<Select>` hst/mad/stl picker with a radiogroup of the
widened `AlgorithmCard` (D-01/D-02/SC3) and swap `DetectorParamGrid`'s raw `<input>` elements for

also carries the phase regression gate.

end-to-end after the rebuild, and SC3 requires the type picker to show the 2px accent-border
selection state (never color alone). The multi-detector model (D-03) is preserved — each detector
block gets its own card picker; the radio-card chooses only that block's type. Depends on 12-01
(widened `AlgorithmCard` + extended `Input`) and, for the full-suite regression gate, on 12-02.

Output: rebuilt `DetectorEntry`/`DetectorParamGrid`, a verified-unchanged `DetectorDisclosure`,
new tests, and a green full build + full vitest suite proving no save/validation regression.
