<objective>
Widen the two Phase 10 shared primitives that the Sensors detector editor (Plan 12-03) must
compose, without introducing any new component. This unblocks D-01/D-02 (reuse `AlgorithmCard`
for the hst/mad/stl radio-card picker) and D-07 (drive `DetectorParamGrid` inline validation
through the shared `Input`).

Purpose: The current `AlgorithmCard.tsx` is over-typed to `DetectorCatalogEntry`/`GroupDetectorName`
(group detectors only) and cannot accept `hst`/`mad`/`stl`; `Input.tsx` lacks the `id`/`step`/
`aria-describedby` passthrough the param grid needs. Both are the correct, spec-aligned fixes
(matching `AlgorithmCard.d.ts` and `Input.d.ts`), not workarounds — see 12-RESEARCH.md Patterns 1
and 4 and Pitfalls 2-4. This is the phase's foundation wave; keeping it separate keeps the blast
radius of the shared-component prop-shape change isolated and independently buildable.

Output: `AlgorithmCard` with generic string props, its single existing call site updated,
`Input` with three additive optional props, and new/updated regression tests.
</objective>
